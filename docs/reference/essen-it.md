The Zentrale IT (ZIT) operates the UME IT. For IT problems, open a ticket in the [ZIT helpdesk](https://helpdesk.uk-essen.de) or use the phone number +49 201 723-4777 (internal 4777). Log in to the helpdesk with your UME email account.

## Guest accounts (EXT)

ZIT sends the login data in an encrypted email to the personal email address from the form. Then do these steps.

1. Log in to [Outlook on the web](https://webcom.ume.de/owa) in 14 days or less.
2. Send an email to the ticket system to tell ZIT about the login.
3. Wait until ZIT activates the two-factor authentication. Then follow the SMS instructions in 24 hours or less.

An EXT account has these limits.

- The address is @ext.uk-essen.de.
- The account operates only in Outlook on the web.
- The account has no access to the global address book or to security groups.
- An email can go to 10 recipients or less.
- The account stops after 12 months. Keno can extend it.

### Rules for remote access to UME data

- Use only the remote access from ZIT. Do not use other routes for data transfer.
- Do your work in areas that are not public. In trains, airports or conference rooms, make sure that other persons cannot see your screen.
- Do not download or keep UME personal data on local drives or removable media.
- Do not keep UME personal data on devices that UME did not give to you.
- Do not print UME personal data.
- Set the automatic screen lock to one minute.
- Do not give your login data to other persons.
- If a security problem occurs or you lose a device, tell ZIT immediately.
- Keep your device safe. Install the current updates of the operating system. Use antivirus software, a firewall and an encrypted WLAN.

## Network on the campus

WPA01 is the UME WLAN with access to the intranet. Only devices that are registered for WPA01 can connect, one device for each account. Do not connect devices that UME did not give to you. The WPA01 form is in the [ZIT intranet](http://intraweb.uk-essen.de/zit/index.php?id=67). eduroam operates with the UDE account and gives access to the internet only.

The Stiftungs-WiFi gives access to the internet without a login. Use it to configure eduroam or other services on a new device.
