subtitle: For personnel with a DKFZ contract

A business trip has four steps. Speak to Keno, send the Dienstreiseantrag, wait for the approval, then make the reservations. Keep all receipts. For unusual costs, speak to the Reisekostenstelle before you make a reservation. Do your first Dienstreiseantrag together with Keno.

## 1. Send the Dienstreiseantrag before the business trip

Send the Dienstreiseantrag in the [Mitarbeiterportal](https://mitarbeiterportal.dkfz-heidelberg.de/).

- For business trips in Germany, send it 5 working days or more before the business trip.
- For business trips to other countries, send it 3 weeks or more before the business trip.

To find the form, select Kalendarium, the key symbol, Reisen and Dienstreise. Write this information in the form.

- The dates and times of the business trip
- The start and the end of the event
- Why the business trip is necessary
- The estimated costs
- The transport

Attach the program or the invitation. Speak to Keno about the cost center. Do not make reservations that you cannot cancel before you have the approval.

## 2. Use the correct reservation procedure

| Item | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Trains in Germany | Get access to the [Bahnportal](https://world.cytric.net/ibe/?system=Bund-VDA) from the Reisekostenstelle. The contact for the group is n.waibel@dkfz-heidelberg.de. You must have the 10-digit Kostenträger 1030000230 and your Personalkennziffer from the payslip. Select Sparpreis Business. Add your BahnCard to the profile. If you do not use a ticket, cancel it immediately. |
| Flights and trains to other countries | Use GBT Deutschland, bund@gbt-bund.de, +49 30 220120 435 (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00). After the approval of a flight, you get a Flugfreigabe email. GBT is in copy. Reply to this email to get the reservation. Economy class is the standard. In the Dienstreiseantrag, write why a flight is necessary. A GBT quote is applicable for a short time only. The GBT service outside the office hours costs more. |
| Hotels | Use the DKFZ HRS link. Keno sends you the link and the password. The link uses the price limits of the federal hotel list (Hotelliste des Bundes, TMS). A hotel with a higher price is permitted only in two conditions. The hotel is the conference hotel, or the listed hotels are more than 20 minutes away by public transport. Then get three quotes and select the hotel with the lowest price. Pay the hotel yourself. The DKFZ gives the reimbursement after the business trip. |
| Hotel invoice | The invoice address is DKFZ, Im Neuenheimer Feld 280, 69120 Heidelberg. This address is also necessary to get the breakfast costs back. |
| Taxi | Use a taxi only if it is necessary, for example if there is no public transport. |

If you add personal days to the business trip, or if a third party pays costs, write this in the Dienstreiseantrag. Speak to the Reisekostenstelle about it before the business trip.

## 3. Send the travel expense claim after the business trip

Send the Reisekostenabrechnung in 6 months or less after you come back. Late claims get no reimbursement. Attach scans of all receipts. Keep the original receipts for 6 months after you send the claim. Write each cost in one claim only.
