The administrative procedures are different for each employer. The site of your work does not change them. If you are not sure who your employer is, read your contract. If your contract changes, follow the procedures of the new employer from the start date.

## DKFZ

| Item | Procedure |
|---|---|
| HR | Speak to Silvia Fuchs (silvia.fuchs@dkfz-heidelberg.de) about contracts, HR onboarding, leave, absence and time recording. |
| Account | Follow the ITCF welcome letter. Change the initial password at [dkfz.de/passwd](https://www.dkfz.de/passwd). Then register the two-factor token. For account problems, send an email to Benutzerverwaltung@dkfz-heidelberg.de. |
| Remote access | Keno starts the VPN procedure in [IDM](https://idm.dkfz.de/). [Webmail](https://webmail.dkfz.de) from outside the DKFZ uses two-factor authentication. |
| Portals | The [intranet](https://intranet.dkfz.de/) has the forms, the contacts and the current instructions. Use the [Mitarbeiterportal](https://mitarbeiterportal.dkfz-heidelberg.de/) (DKFZ AD login) for business trips and their approval status. |
| Business trips | Refer to [DKFZ business trips](dkfz-travel.md). |
| Mandatory training | E-learning courses of DKFZ Advanced Training (M300). Do them each year. Data protection takes about 30 minutes. AI competence takes 45 to 60 minutes. Prevention of corruption is the third course. |
| Purchases | Speak to Keno about the purchase and the funding. Keno makes the purchase in e.biss (Bestellanforderung, Banf). For software that is not in the e.biss catalog (for example BioRender), the vendor must first send a quote. For Apple hardware, IT purchasing (it-einkauf@dkfz-heidelberg.de) gets the quote. For IT purchases of more than 25,000 EUR, the EDV-Ausschuss form is also necessary. |
| Benefits | Personnel with a DKFZ contract can use EGYM Wellpass (company fitness). Register through the [DKFZ intranet](https://intranet.dkfz.de/). |
| Grants | Each grant application with DKFZ affiliation must have the approval of the board. Send these documents to Joel de Blois, Grants Office (joel.deblois@dkfz.de), 14 days or more before the deadline. The documents are the checklist, the budget, the list of partners, the title, the duration and an abstract of one page. For TUM or UME affiliation, use the procedures of TUM or UME. |

## Universitätsmedizin Essen (UME)

| Item | Procedure |
|---|---|
| HR | Personalbetreuung, Dez. 01.1, Hufelandstraße 55, Essen. |
| Account | UME makes the user ID and the address firstname.lastname@uk-essen.de with the contract. Refer to [Essen IT](essen-it.md). |
| Forms | Most UME forms are in Roxtra. You can open Roxtra through [home.ume.de](https://home.ume.de) from the UME network. |
| Sickness | Tell Keno as early as possible. Set an automatic out-of-office reply. From the fourth calendar day, you must have a medical certificate. |
| Leave | Use the leave form "Urlaubsantrag" from [Roxtra (file 169979)](https://roxtra.uk-essen.de/Roxtra/index.aspx?fileid=169979). Use the leave days from the last year before 30 September (scientific personnel) or 31 May (other personnel). |
| Business trips | Use SAP Concur for the travel application, the approval and the reimbursement. Log in with your UME email address. The UME travel agency makes the reservations. Send your travel expense claim to Reisekostenstelle@uk-essen.de in 6 months or less after the business trip. The invoice address for conferences is Universitätsklinikum Essen, Rechnungsbüro, Hufelandstr. 55, 45147 Essen. The rules are in [Roxtra (file 278277)](https://roxtra.uk-essen.de/Roxtra/index.aspx?fileid=278277). |
| Mandatory training | Do the Pflichtschulungen as e-learning in the [Bildungsakademie LMS](https://bildungsakademie-online.ume.de/). Do them again after one to three years. The courses include fire safety, hygiene, data protection, information security, occupational safety, patient safety, compliance, equal treatment (AGG) and sustainability. |
| Secondary employment | If you have a paid secondary employment, send the form from [Roxtra (file 30983)](https://roxtra.uk-essen.de/Roxtra/index.aspx?fileid=30983) to HR before it starts. |
| Code of conduct | All personnel must obey the UME code of conduct (Roxtra file 84779). |
